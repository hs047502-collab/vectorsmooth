            Questions

            Short answers, no marketing fog.

            Will it ever change my design?

            No. Five locks enforce it: a movement budget on every stage, neighbour-clearance caps so paths can never merge, a separation backstop, a detail lock that keeps holes and cutouts intact, and render-vs-render QA gates. If anything breaches, you get your original file back untouched — and the Pixel QA tab shows the proof (IoU, chamfer, dev-vs-budget). A borderline IoU on its own never discards good work: when every other lock verifies the file clean, the smoothing is kept and the report says so.

            Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

            Never. The whole engine runs inside your browser (Python via WebAssembly). Only the one-time engine download needs internet; your artwork stays on your device.

            SAFE vs PRO mode?

            SAFE is marketplace clean — fidelity never drops below 80, maximum caution. PRO optimizes harder (fewer nodes) under the same hard movement budget. Both modes rollback on any violation.

            What can I export?

            Clean SVG plus true EPS level 8 or 10 with structural validation and a marketplace preflight on every file. Single files or the whole batch as one ZIP (the EPS batch includes a preflight.txt report), folder structure preserved.

            Will marketplaces accept my EPS files?

            Every EPS download carries a marketplace preflight: file structure (DSC header, RGB paints, Illustrator 8/10-compatible operators, no fonts, no raster), artwork size, and per-design blockers — open paths, strokes, live text, transparency, gradients, clipping. Fix what it flags and the file uploads error-free to Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Vecteezy and Freepik. Note: strokes and text must be expanded to outlines in Illustrator — the tool flags them but never rewrites your design on its own.

            Does it work offline / from a file?

            Yes — this page is one self-contained file. Double-click it and it works; after the first engine download it also survives flaky connections (the engine stays cached).
